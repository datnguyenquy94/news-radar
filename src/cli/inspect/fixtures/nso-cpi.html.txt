				<div class="fusion-fullwidth fullwidth-box fusion-builder-row-1 nonhundred-percent-fullwidth non-hundred-percent-height-scrolling" style="--awb-border-radius-top-left:0px;--awb-border-radius-top-right:0px;--awb-border-radius-bottom-right:0px;--awb-border-radius-bottom-left:0px;" ><div class="fusion-builder-row fusion-row"><div class="fusion-layout-column fusion_builder_column fusion-builder-column-0 fusion_builder_column_1_1 1_1 fusion-one-full fusion-column-first fusion-column-last" style="--awb-bg-size:cover;"><div class="fusion-column-wrapper fusion-flex-column-wrapper-legacy"><div class="fusion-text fusion-text-1"><p style="text-align: justify;">The Consumer Price Index (CPI) in June 2026 recorded a month-on-month decrease of 0.39%, mainly attributable to a decline in gasoline and oil prices in line with global fuel price movements. Compared to December 2025, the CPI in June increased by 3.21% and rose by 4.69% year-on-year.</p>
<p>a) Consumer Price Index</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The CPI in June fell by 0.39% compared to the previous month, with price indices declining in four groups of goods and services, while increasing in seven groups. On average, the CPI in the second quarter of 2026 rose by 5.25% year-on-year. The average CPI for the first six months of 2026 increased by 4.38% compared to the same period last year.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">Core inflation in June posted a month-on-month increase of 0.14% and a year-on-year rise of 4.50%. On average, core inflation in the first six months of 2026 went up by 4.12% year-on-year, lower than the 4.38% increase of the overall average CPI.</p>
<p>b) Gold and US dollar price indices</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The gold price index in June declined by 8.46% compared to May; increased by 24.11% year-on-year; and decreased by 2.72% compared to December 2025. On average, the gold price index in the second quarter of 2026 rose by 36.84% year-on-year, while the average index for the first six months of 2026 surged by 58.12% compared to the same period last year.</p>
<p style="text-align: justify;">The US dollar price index in June recorded a month-on-month increase of 0.11%; rose by 0.57% year-on-year; and fell by 0.17% compared to December 2025. On average, the US dollar price index in the second quarter of 2026 increased by 0.93% year-on-year, and the average index for the first six months of 2026 climbed by 1.75%.</p>

<p>In the context of increasing industrialization and modernization, Vietnam is committed to implementing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), in which protecting water resources and the environment is a top priority, especially wastewater treatment, which has become a major challenge. <span>(14/01/2025)</span></p>
<h3><a href="https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/events/2023/01/press-conference-to-announce-socio-economic-statistics-fourth-quarter-and-2022/">Press conference to announce socio-economic statistics fourth quarter and 2022</a></h3>
<p>On the morning of December 29, 2022, the General Statistics Office (GSO) held a press conference to announce the socio-economic statistics of the fourth quarter and 2022. Ms. Nguyen Thi Huong, General Director of the General Statistics Office chaired conference. At the press conference, Ms. Nguyen Thi Huong announced the basic issues about the socio-economic situation of Vietnam in the fourth quarter and 2022. <span>(29/12/2022)</span></p>
<h3><a href="https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/other-news/2022/11/2023-iaos-prize-for-young-statisticians/">2023 IAOS Prize for Young Statisticians</a></h3>
<p>The International Association for Official Statistics (IAOS) is pleased to announce the launch of the 2023 IAOS Young Statisticians Prize. This international prize encourages young statisticians to take an active interest in official statistics and is awarded for the best paper in the field of official statistics written by a young statistician. <span>(18/11/2022)</span></p>
<h3><a href="https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/other-news/2022/11/action-plan-on-central-highlands-development-issued/">Action plan on Central Highlands development issued</a></h3>
<p>VGP - The Government has recently issued Resolution 152/NQ-CP (Resolution 152) approving an action plan to implement Resolution 23-NQ-TW of the Poliburo on the orientations for socio-economic development and defence-security safeguarding in the Central Highlands by 2030 with a vision towards 2045. <span>(16/11/2022)</span></p>
<h3><a href="https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/other-news/2022/11/three-vietnamese-seaports-among-top-100-largest-container-ports-worldwide/">Three Vietnamese seaports among TOP 100 largest container ports worldwide</a></h3>
<p>VGP - Viet Nam has three seaports in the top 50 seaports with the largest cargo throughput in the world, according to the latest update to the list of 100 seaports by Lloyd Maritime Company (UK). <span>(14/11/2022)</span></p>
<h3><a href="https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/other-news/2019/11/vietnams-gdp-revision-poses-no-change-to-short-term-state-budgetary-strategy/">Vietnam’s GDP revision poses no change to short-term state budgetary strategy</a></h3>
